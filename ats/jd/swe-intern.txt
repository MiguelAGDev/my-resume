Software Engineering Intern

Composite of typical internship requirements from Google, Meta and Deere entry-level postings.

Minimum qualifications:
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree program in Computer Science or a related technical field
- Experience with data structures, algorithms, and software design
- Experience in at least one programming language such as Python, Java, C++, JavaScript, or C#
- Familiarity with object-oriented programming and version control (Git)
- Experience working in Unix/Linux environments

Preferred qualifications:
- Previous internship, work experience, personal projects, open source, or coding competition experience
- Experience with SQL and relational databases
- Experience building or consuming REST APIs
- Experience with automated testing and CI/CD
- Ability to collaborate in an Agile team and communicate clearly
